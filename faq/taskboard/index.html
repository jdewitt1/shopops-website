TaskBoard FAQ

Delegated operations questions.

Answers about the coming soon TaskBoard platform and managed Shopify operations workflows.

Is TaskBoard available now?

TaskBoard is coming soon. Preview access and workflow discussions can be requested through ShopOps.

What workflow does it use?

The core workflow is Triage -> Solve -> Verify -> Complete, designed for delegated Shopify operations.

Is this only a task app?

No. TaskBoard is intended as operational infrastructure for managed services, app workflows, and centralized merchant visibility.

Will the app install be free?

The current direction is a free app install connected to operational subscriptions and managed services.

Who completes the work?

ShopOps operators handle delegated work, while merchants retain visibility into status and approvals.

Can it support multiple stores?

Multi-store operational support is part of the Enterprise services direction and future platform planning.
