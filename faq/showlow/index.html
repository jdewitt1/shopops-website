ShowLow FAQ

Low inventory visibility questions.

Answers for merchants evaluating inventory urgency and merchandising workflows.

What does ShowLow monitor?

ShowLow focuses on products approaching low inventory thresholds so teams can act before sellout.

Can thresholds be customized?

Threshold assumptions can be configured around catalog behavior and merchant operating preferences.

Does it automatically create promotions?

The current marketing direction is to identify and promote low inventory products; exact automation can be configured through setup and future documentation.

Who should use it?

Merchants with meaningful catalog movement, seasonal inventory, or products where scarcity should inform merchandising.

Can ShopOps manage the workflow?

Yes. Managed services can include recurring review, threshold tuning, and merchandising execution.

Documentation is currently a placeholder. Request the latest setup guidance through support.
