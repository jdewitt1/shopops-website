PreOrders FAQ

Pre-order operations questions.

Answers for merchants evaluating purchase order aware pre-orders.

How does PreOrders decide what can be sold?

The intended workflow uses incoming inventory and purchase order context to determine eligible products and quantities.

Can it prevent overselling?

It is designed to reduce oversell risk with rule-based guardrails, but merchants should still maintain accurate inventory and PO data.

Do I have to toggle every product manually?

No. The product direction is dynamic pre-order enablement based on operational data and configured rules.

Does it work with variants?

Variant-level scenarios are part of the intended operating model for products with different stock positions.

Can ShopOps set it up?

Yes. ShopOps can assist with installation, configuration, QA, and ongoing support.

Where is documentation?

Documentation is currently a placeholder. Request the latest setup guidance through support.
