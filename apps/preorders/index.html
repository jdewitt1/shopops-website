Merchant problem

Stop babysitting pre-order switches.

When demand arrives before stock does, merchants need a controlled way to sell against incoming inventory. ShopOps PreOrders is designed to reduce manual rule changes, prevent sloppy overselling, and make purchase order timing more useful inside Shopify.

Features

Dynamic pre-order enablement for operational teams.

Connect inventory expectations to storefront availability with rules that scale across products and variants.

Incoming inventory logic

Use expected replenishment to determine when a product can safely accept pre-orders.

Purchase order aware

Plan pre-order availability around known purchase order quantities and timing.

Oversell controls

Reduce risk with guardrails that keep pre-order volume aligned with incoming stock.

No manual toggles

Replace one-off edits with structured rules and operational workflows.

Variant support

Account for product and variant-level inventory scenarios.

Merchant visibility

Give operators clearer context for why pre-orders are active.

Workflow

From purchase order to pre-order availability.

1

Import signals

Bring incoming inventory and purchase order context into the workflow.

2

3

Publish state

4

Monitor risk

Keep pre-order activity aligned with inventory expectations.

Does this replace inventory planning?

No. It helps operationalize incoming inventory data inside Shopify so pre-order decisions are easier to manage.

Can ShopOps help configure it?

Yes. ShopOps can support installation, rules, QA, and ongoing operational management.
